A curiosity for the world.

My research interests sit at the intersection of Earth observation and artificial intelligence: how can models connect what we see, what we describe, and where we are?

I study Remote Sensing Science and Technology at the School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University (Class of 2024). My research experience spans dataset construction, model implementation, and paper revision. I enjoy turning ideas into code and learning through hands-on experiments.
